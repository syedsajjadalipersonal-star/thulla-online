How Thulla works
  1. All 52 cards are dealt, 13 each. Whoever holds the A♠ opens the first trick with it. Play goes clockwise.
  2. Everyone must follow the suit that was led. Ace is high, 2 is low.
  3. If everyone follows, the trick goes to the discard pile and the highest card leads next.
  4. On the first round there are no thullas: if you have no spades, play any card and it goes to the pile with the rest of the trick.
  5. After that, if you can't follow, play any card. That's a thulla. The trick stops right there and whoever played the highest card of the led suit picks up every card on the table, then leads.
  6. Empty your hand and you're safe. The last player still holding cards loses.
